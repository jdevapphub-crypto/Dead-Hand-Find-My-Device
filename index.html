HOW THE TRACKER WORKS

1. WHAT GETS TRACKED

  • GPS location — sent to our Firebase database.
  • Last active time — when the phone last reported in.
  • Battery level — so you know if the phone is about to die.
  • Charging state — plugged in or on battery.
  • Network status — WiFi or mobile data.

2. HOW OFTEN

  • Normal mode: every 30 seconds.
  • Lost mode: every 15 seconds.
  • Ring command: immediate — wakes the phone and forces a fresh GPS read.

3. WHEN THE PHONE IS OFFLINE

The phone writes its last known location to Firebase every time it has internet. If it goes offline (airplane mode, dead battery, network down), the last position stays in the cloud. The moment the phone reconnects, it sends its new location within seconds.

4. LIVE STATUS MEANINGS

🟢 Online Reporting now
🟡 Recent Active in last hour
⚪ Offline No signal for over an hour

5. RING MY DEVICE

If a phone is lost, you can ring it remotely. The phone wakes up, plays a loud alarm on full volume, and shows a full-screen alert that only the holder can silence. The ring also forces a fresh GPS read.

6. REPORT AS LOST

Flag a device as LOST in one tap. The last known location is stored, and you'll be notified the moment the phone comes back online.

7. LIMITS — THE HONEST TRUTH

  • If the phone is off, GPS is off — nothing can track it.
  • If the phone is offline, only the last known position is available.
  • If the phone is factory reset or the app is deleted, tracking stops.

ABOUT DEAD HAND

DEAD HAND is a personal safety app built in South Africa. It notifies your trusted people if you do not check in — and helps you find a lost or stolen phone.

WHAT IT DOES

  • Safety Mode — check-in timer, SMS alerts if you don't check in.
  • Safe Zones — trusted places, alert if you're away too long.
  • Group Safety Mode — up to 7 people tracking each other live.
  • Find My Device — locate someone by their code.
  • Ring My Device — remote ring a lost phone.
  • Report as Lost — get notified when it comes online.

WHO IT'S FOR

Commuters, hikers, students, parents, elderly — anyone who goes places where a check-in matters.

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 2026

1. WHAT WE COLLECT

  • GPS location — used to show your position to people you trust.
  • Email — used only to sign you in.
  • Display name — shown to your trusted contacts and code lookups.
  • Device identifier — a random ID stored on your phone.
  • Contacts you add — codes and phone numbers of your chosen partners.
  • FCM token — to deliver push notifications.

2. WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES

Google Firebase (United States, us-central1).

3. WHO CAN SEE YOUR DATA

  • You.
  • Anyone you share your unique code with.
  • Your trusted partners.
  • BulkSMS — receives phone numbers to send SMS alerts.
  • Google Firebase — hosts the data.

We never sell your data. We never share it with advertisers.

4. LOCATION DATA

Only accessed while the app is open or a safety feature is enabled. You can turn off any feature at any time.

5. YOUR RIGHTS

You can delete your account, remove partners, remove Safe Zones, and request a copy of your data. Contact: deadhandjoheliv@gmail.com

6. CHILDREN

DEAD HAND is not intended for users under 16.
